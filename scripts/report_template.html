Ridgeline Schools Weather Network · health check

Can we trust this spring's weather data, and where should the technician go?

Eight stations, [[period]], a reading every 10 minutes. Prepared for the network coordinator.
1 · Which data can go in the spring summary?
Almost all of it. Two series are not usable after 26 March.

Of the [[n_series]] station-measurements (8 stations × 7 measurements), [[clean_series]] are good exactly as sent and [[fixed_series]] are good after I corrected them (S06's clock and S02's pressure). [[part_series]] have a short hole that I left out, and [[bad_series]] should not be used for the rest of the period:

  • S04 afternoon temperature since 26 Mar
  • S07 wind speed since 26 Mar
2 · Where should the technician go next week?
S04, Foothills Community Garden.

Its afternoon temperature has crept up to [[s04_last]] °C too warm and is still growing, which would spoil the headline number of a climate summary. Nobody can fix that without being there.

One hour: check the temperature shield and the white-painted post, compare with a hand thermometer, and look at the rain gauge cleaned on 2 April.

Why S04 and not S07

  • Temperature is the number the summary is about; wind speed is a side measurement.
  • S07 can be settled by a photo. S04 cannot: the cause is on the post.
  • S04 gets worse each sunny week, so waiting costs more data than at S07, where the loss is already total but not growing.
Left-out stretches are holes, not guesses. Nothing was filled in; averages there use the readings that remain. S01 on the ridge was healthy: its 60 km/h gust on 14 Mar was a real storm six other stations felt too, and the 100 % humidity on 30 Mar (S05, S06) is real saturation, so both stay in.

Where I am less certain
  • The S04 drift may have started on 15 Mar, when the post was repainted, but it only becomes clear in late March; I excluded from the date the data show it.
  • S02's correction assumes the new board is off by a constant amount; only calibration would prove it.
  • S07's factor is a hunch from the numbers until someone reads the label.
